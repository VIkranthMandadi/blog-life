I should probably start naming these posts something a little more interesting than the number that the post is, but anyways. Yesterday I worked from home. It was pretty great. I got to sleep in. I went on a few sidequests. Finally, I decided I was on an adventure to make some form of chicken pasta. The idea started with a nice slow cooker recipe, but then I did not want to wait that long, so I thought, all good, I can just make it on the stove. However, I wanted to add my own twist to it, so I thought I would bake the chicken. I got the baking sheet all oiled up, cut some semi thawed chicken, seasoned it up, and tossed that in the oven.

Then I looked in the pantry and realized we had no pasta sauce! Oh boy. So I had to go to Walmart; I kicked off a little task with Grok while I made my way there. I decided on some garlic-something sauce, bought that, some macaroni salad (which really hurt my stomach as always), and something else that I don't remember now. I made my way home, cooked the pasta while checking on Grok, tossed in some frozen veggies, the sauce, and the cut up baked chicken. Then I went to add some Italian seasoning. I accidentally opened the wrong side of the jar, and poof, dumped more in than I expected. Honestly, not bad, but definitely could be better. Well, one thing I learned yesterday: one pack of pasta makes way more pasta than I expected, so now I have a lot of chicken garlic-something pasta in my fridge. And by the end of it all, Grok came to the conclusion that there might not have been a solution to the issue that it was working on while I made my pasta. How great is that — thanks, Grok!

Fun Fact:

A random fact I learned yesterday. Philosopher David Henry Thoreau reversed his first and middle name to Henry David Thoreau, and now pretty much everyone references him as Henry. He also used to go on very long walks, but he would refer to them as sauntering, an art of wandering with no destination or practical purpose. Next time anyone asks me to walk faster I will let them know that I am going on a nice saunter to be present in nature (or I am just tired and want to walk slow).
