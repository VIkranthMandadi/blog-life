Yesterday was pretty interesting. Bob and I woke up pretty early to go on our long run. The sunrise was a beautiful orange and the temperature was as good as can be for Texas: a peaceful morning. I was planning to do 16 miles and said he would run the first 10 with me and then wait till I finished. Long story short at mile 8 Bob decided to keep running with me and he finished up at 14 mi and me at 15.5: we were both extremely sore after.

After taking a shower we decided to go to Costco. Naturally, we were trying as many of the free samples as posisble. One of the samples happened to be protein ice cream from a brand called Frozen One (https://www.frozen-one.com/), and it was pretty good. We were not planning on getting it but after trying the ice cream and stepping away one of the people manning the station, who I thought was the sales rep, asked us how we liked the ice cream. We both said it was pretty good and he mentioned that they were trying to get this product in Costco and to do so they needed to sell X amount of ice cream that day. He said that if we bought 20 boxes he would send us the money for those 20 boxes, so we ended up walking out of Costco that day with 20 boxes of ice cream. On the way out we got a lot of funny looks and comments about how much Bob and I like ice cream ut honestly its pretty solid. We also learned the supposed sales rep was actually the 26 year old CEO of the company Frozen One and thye were expanding quick after recetly raising 5 million dollars of VC funding, pretty impressive.

All in all shoutout Frozen One. If you have not tried it yet this is not a paid sponsor, well I guess they did buy us ice cream, but highly recommend it is pretty solid.

The rest of the day just streteched and laid in bed while my legs ached from the run.
