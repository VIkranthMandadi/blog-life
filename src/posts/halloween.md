Haven't been on here for a while, but here is a quick overdue update. Honestly, life has been crazy and monotonous at the same time. I was pretty sick last week so I took some time to rest, but excited to get back at it.

The most eventful weekend over the last few weeks was definitely Halloween. I went to California to spend time with my girlfriend and her friends, and it was really great to see her. San Francisco always seems like it should be my place as I work in tech, but it still always feels like the dystopian future with the AI billboards, AI signs on buses, and the tech culture in general. But at the same time there is a beauty to the hustle. The weather was great, the food was great, and the Ghirardelli peppermint hot chocolate + sundae combo was really great.

We went to a couple of bars Friday and Saturday. The two we went to, Westwood and Jaxons, were pretty fun, but it felt like the average club/bar vibe. There was a cover at every bar and the music was alright, maybe a solid 5/10 going out experience. Madison, WI bars trump this for sure.

The one place that really impressed me was the Palace of Fine Arts. I had never been there before and it was so beautiful. I enjoyed walking around looking at the flowers and talking to my girlfriend in the peace and serenity of the park, although her voice would break the peace pretty consistently 😉. I joke, I joke, it was a great time.

No fun facts today. I am trying to keep this post short, and even though there have been so many crazy things in the news, I think we can save that for the coming days.
