I decided to name this post double dinner: pretty straightforward. At work, boss, big boss, and big big boss were all out of office, so most of the team worked from home, including me. Post work though, Bob and I decided to go to the gym and get dinner after. I swam for the first time in ages, and it was terrible, absolutely awful. I was in the water for about 10 minutes before I felt tired, but I am pretty determined to get better. Next time, i am thinking 15 with half the amount of water swalled.

For dinner we wanted to try this pho place (Pho CUI), but the line was pretty long so we went to a ramen place. Then we went to REI to acquire some gels for our morning run, but afterward Bob said we should get some tacos. Back to back dinner action. We went to Tacos El Charly, honestly some pretty insane tacos with unlimited toppings, and Bob went heavy on the cucumbers. I got two tacos, chorizo and al pastor, and a horchata. I was extremely full, and someone (no names) even called me large for eating that much. Bob got three tacos.

The one challenge about that taco place was they announced numbers in Spanish, yelling "Cincuenta y seis" for example. I am not gonna lie, I really had to focus and bring back my high school Spanish, but at the end of the day we got our food, a big win and highly recommended. If you really like cucumbers as toppings, Bob highly recommends the place too.
